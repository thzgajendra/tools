Free Email List Checker

Paste one address per line (or comma separated). The check runs entirely in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere.

Need to know if the domain really receives mail? This page only checks what can be known offline. The Email List Checker on Apify adds a live mail-server (MX) lookup and a list of 120,000+ disposable domains, at $1 per 1,000 addresses, with an API and spreadsheet export.

What the statuses mean

  • valid: well formed, no warning signs found here.
  • risky: well formed, but a role account (info@, sales@), a likely typo of a common provider, or a known throwaway domain.
  • invalid: not a well-formed address.

This tool does not contact mail servers and cannot confirm a mailbox exists. Its disposable list is a short sample. Check only lists you have a lawful reason to hold. Built by Gajendra Malviya, gajendramalviya.me.
