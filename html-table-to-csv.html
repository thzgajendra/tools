Free HTML Table to CSV Converter

On any web page, right-click a table, choose Inspect, copy the <table> element (or copy the whole page source) and paste it below. Pick the table, then copy or download the CSV. Runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

FAQ

Is my HTML uploaded? No. Processing is local JavaScript.

What about merged cells? colspan/rowspan are not expanded; each cell becomes one column in its row.

Nested tables? Only the outer table's text is kept, inner tables are flattened into the cell text.
